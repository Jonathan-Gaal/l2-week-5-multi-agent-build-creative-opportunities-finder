Artist category is a stand-in: each listing was read and given the discipline it suits, until the Extractor does this. “Needs review” marks listings that look promotional, ask for panelists, or are surveys rather than calls to apply. Open mics are recurring: the deadline column shows the next date or the weekly time, and each mic appears once. Gigs are listings from Craigslist and Playbill Jobs. “Event” is Jon's list only: a workshop, class, jam or dance night, something to attend rather than apply for. “Multiple disciplines” marks listings Jon tagged with more than one discipline, mostly mixed open mics. “Found by” says which list a listing came from; “Both” means both lists have it. Jon's list also holds records from outside NYC, which are left out here. Past deadlines are removed.
